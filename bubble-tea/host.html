珍珠奶茶搶答用平板掃描 QR code 就能玩 · Scan to play

房號 Room----

即時排行榜 Live leaderboard

怎麼玩 How to play

  1. 拿起平板，打開相機或 QR 掃描器，對準左邊的 QR code。 Open the camera or a QR scanner on the tablet and point it at the QR code.
  2. 掃到的人會自動加入房號 ，成績即時顯示在右邊的排行榜。換一個班級就按「新房間」。 Everyone who scans joins the same room, and their scores appear live on the board. Tap "New room" for the next class.
  3. 輸入暱稱、選一個頭像。 Type a nickname and pick an avatar.
  4. 每題都有中文和英文，看得懂哪一個都可以作答。想讓英文放大，按「英語在前 English first」。 Every question is in Chinese and English. Tap "English first" to make English the big line.
  5. 答得越快分數越高，連續答對還有連勝加分。 Answer faster for more points; correct streaks earn a bonus.
  6. 最後會出現成績、每一關的表現，還有全部題目的中英文解析，可以直接當複習。 At the end everyone gets a score and a bilingual review of every question.
